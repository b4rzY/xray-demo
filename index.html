About the studio & credits

The mirror fits a reference skull to visible facial landmarks. It cannot see through skin, measure your bones, or identify a medical condition. The hand overlay illustrates finger positions, not the shape of your bones.

Elemental mode reacts to a palm, fist, pinch, paired palms or open mouth. Demo gestures use synthetic inputs. Camera mode downloads Google's tracking runtime and models. Inference runs in your browser; this app does not record or upload camera frames. Stop the camera at any time. The camera also stops when you leave the tab.

Use the arrow keys in the viewport to orbit, + or − to zoom, and Home to reset. The section control removes outer anatomy progressively. In live mode, hold a thumb–index pinch and move it sideways to adjust the section.
