Inside the atlas

Reference geometry

The skull and dentition are adapted from Dr. Mariano Coretti’s dental anatomy model. Its adapted geometry is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. See mesh attribution and changes. The cerebral and facial muscle layers are procedurally illustrated, not scan-derived. The hand skeleton uses illustrative segments mapped to visible landmarks.

Live tracking

Camera mode uses Google's Face Landmarker and Hand Landmarker. It downloads MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.21 and its WebAssembly runtime from jsDelivr, plus version 1 float16 model files from Google Cloud Storage. No software or models from those providers load in the camera-off demo. Camera frames are processed in the browser, not sent to the studio.
