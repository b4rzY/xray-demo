Adaptations: VTK connected-component separation into upper cranium,
mandible, upper dentition and lower dentition. Right-handed orientation
(x, z, -y) and uniform scaling, then VTK quadric decimation with volume
preservation and vertex normal computation. These are adapted educational
reference meshes, not measurements of the person viewing the app.

Format (.xrm; little-endian): uint32 vertex count then uint32 index
count. Next, six float32 values per vertex: position x, y, z followed
by normal x, y, z. Last, zero-based uint32 triangle indices (three per
triangle). Four files total: skull.xrm (83,640 triangles), mandible.xrm
(13,278), teeth.xrm (12,462), lower-teeth.xrm (13,578).
