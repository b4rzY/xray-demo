Anatomy credits

The 3D skull and dentition are adapted from High quality skull.stl by Dr. Mariano Coretti, from the University of L’Aquila dentistry thesis Study and implementation of a decomposable virtual skull in 54 anatomically correct elements.

The original geometry and adapted .xrm mesh assets are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0. The mesh was reoriented, rescaled, grouped for a movable jaw and separate dentition, decimated, and converted to an indexed binary format. Lighting and materials were added for this presentation.

The brain and muscle overlays are original procedural illustrations, not clinical scans. The anatomy is not measured from your face. This is an educational visual effect, not an X-ray or diagnostic tool.
